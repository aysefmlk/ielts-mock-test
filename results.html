What to do next

  • Listening below band 6: the problem is usually Parts 3 and 4. Re-listen to those two parts with the transcript open and mark every point where you lost the thread.
  • Reading below band 6: check whether you ran out of time or got the answers wrong. They need different remedies. Time trouble is a skimming problem; wrong answers on True/False/Not Given are usually a problem with the difference between "contradicted" and "not mentioned".
  • Writing: count how many of your Task 2 body paragraphs contain a specific example. Most answers stuck at band 5.5 are made of general statements.
  • Speaking: time three of your own Part 3 answers. If they average under 25 seconds, fluency is costing you more than grammar is.
