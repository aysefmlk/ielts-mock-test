Before you begin

  • Sit the modules in order and without a break if you want a realistic result. In the real test, Listening, Reading and Writing run back to back with no pause between them.
  • Spelling counts. In Listening and Reading an answer with a spelling mistake is marked wrong. Both British and American spellings are accepted.
  • Respect the word limits. "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" means an answer of three words scores zero even if the content is right.
  • Your work is saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the tab and come back. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
